# FINDRELATIVEMONTH

**Kategori:** LG - Logo islem/guncelleme proseduru (toplam guncelleme, referans cozumleme vb.).

**Ad kalibi:** `LG_FINDRELATIVEMONTH_{XXX}_{YY}`

## Parametreler

| # | Parametre | Tip | Yon |
|--:|-----------|-----|-----|
| 1 | `@TRDATE` | datetime | IN |
| 2 | `@RETMON` | smallint | OUTPUT |
| 3 | `@RETYEAR` | smallint | OUTPUT |
